04 / How it works

From dirty to done in about 20 minutes.

No appointment needed for single washes. Booking reserves a bay at a fixed time.

Step 1

Rinse inspection

We rinse the car and photograph pre existing damage with you. We agree the service before anything is charged.

3 min
Step 2

Wash and vacuum

Foam bath and pressure rinse outside. Full vacuum, glass, and wipe down inside. Bays 1 and 2.

10 to 15 min
Step 3

Hand dry and finish

Spot free hand dry, glass polish, tyre dressing. Final check under the bay lights.

5 min
Step 4

Walk around, then pay

You inspect the car with us. Any miss is rewashed free on the spot. You pay after.

2 min

Terms of Service

1. Payment is taken after the final walk around. If you are not satisfied we rewash once at no charge.

2. Single wash bookings can be cancelled free of charge until 2 hours before. Monthly plans cancel at the register and run to month end.

3. We photograph pre existing damage before washing. We do not cover damage documented at inspection.

4. Vehicles must be collected within 24 hours. Storage after that is $10 per day.

Privacy Policy

1. We store your name, phone number, plate, and booking time to run reservations and SMS confirmations.

2. We do not sell personal data. Cameras cover the bays for safety and footage is deleted after 14 days.

3. To view or delete your data, call +1 (555) 010-2030 or ask at the register.
